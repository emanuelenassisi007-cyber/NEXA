Frequently asked questions The essentials, at a glance.
How do I add a new customer?
Open "Customers" from the sidebar and select the "Add customer" button. Fill out the form, then save the record.
How do I edit or delete a customer?
Open "Customers" from the sidebar and select the "Delete customer" button. Fill out the email form, then click on "Delete" button.
How do I create a new order?
Open "Orders" from the sidebar and select the "New Order" button. You will be redirected to the page for adding a new order: select products from the menu on the left by clicking the "Add" button, choose the required quantity for each product, and click the "Complete Order" button. The site will display a summary page with the details of the newly confirmed order, including the option to download the invoice.
How can I search and filter my data?
Use the search bar to find a specific record, or use the available filters to narrow down the results. Depending on the section, you can filter data by fields such as name, status, date, or other available criteria.
How do I export my data?
Open "Settings" from the sidebar and scroll down to the "User Profile" section. Click the "Export my data" button. The file will be downloaded to your PC in Excel format.
How do I change my password?
Open "Settings" from the sidebar and scroll down to the "User Profile" section. Click the "Change password" button. You will be redirected to the password reset page. From there, follow the instructions.
How do I enable or disable two-factor authentication?
Open "Settings" from the sidebar and scroll down to the "Security" section. Click the "Two-factor authentication" switch. Follow the on-screen instructions.
How do notifications work?
You can choose which notifications to receive by going to the "Settings" page via the side menu and scrolling down to the "Notifications" section. There, you can select whether or not to receive email notifications. Website and push notifications are enabled by default. You can choose which events trigger notifications—such as orders, new users or customers, or account changes.
